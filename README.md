# 📊 Global Financial Markets & Sector Performance Dashboard

An interactive, modern Excel dashboard designed to analyze and visualize global financial market trends, sector variations, company-level stock performance, and the market impact of major financial news events.

---

## 📸 Dashboard Preview

![Dashboard Screenshot](dashboard_screenshot.png)


## 🚀 Key Highlights & Metrics

* *Core KPIs*: High-level overview tracking *Average Trading Volume*, *Market Average Return*, *Top Performing Sector*, and *Top Stock*.
* *Market Impact by News Events*: Analysis of trading volume spikes and index shifts triggered by global financial/economic news.
* *Sector & Company Breakdown*: Horizontal distribution highlighting performance across various economic sectors and individual equity assets.
* *Interactive Dynamic Slicers*: Multi-dimensional filtering by timeline/month and market sentiment (Positive, Neutral, Negative).

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Design Approach

* *Tool*: Microsoft Excel
* *Techniques*: Pivot Tables, Dynamic Cell References, Pivot Charts, Custom Slicers.
* *UI/UX Design*: Built with a clean *Flat/Card Layout* for clear hierarchy, distraction-free visual flow, and high data contrast.
